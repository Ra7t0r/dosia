Common questions

My reminders don't arrive.
Check that notifications are allowed for Dosia in iPhone Settings → Notifications → Dosia, and that Focus modes are not silencing them.
The widget shows old numbers.
Open Dosia once so the widget refreshes. If it still looks wrong, remove the widget and add it again.
Will I lose my data if I delete the app?
Yes. Your data is stored only on your iPhone, so deleting Dosia deletes it. It may be restored from an iPhone backup made before you deleted the app.

Privacy Policy

Last updated: 18 October 2026

Dosia is built so that your health information stays with you. This policy explains what the app stores and what it doesn't do.

What Dosia stores

  • The profile details you enter: name, avatar, weight, height and activity level, used to calculate your daily water goal.
  • Your water intake entries.
  • Your medications, their schedules and the doses you log.
  • Your app settings, such as reminders and appearance.

All of this is stored only on your device. It is shared between the app and its widgets on the same iPhone so the widgets can show your progress.

What Dosia does not do

  • It does not send your data to any server. The app has no account and no sign-in.
  • It does not use analytics, advertising or tracking tools.
  • It does not sell or share your data with anyone.
  • It does not read from or write to Apple Health.

Notifications

Reminders are scheduled locally on your iPhone. You can turn them off in the app or in iPhone Settings at any time.

Backups

If you use iCloud Backup or back up your iPhone to a computer, Apple may include Dosia's data in that backup, as with any app. This is handled by Apple under Apple's privacy policy; the developer has no access to it.

Contacting the developer

If you email for support, your email address and message are used only to reply to you and are not shared.

Deleting your data

Delete the Dosia app from your iPhone to remove all of its data from the device.

Children

Dosia is not directed at children and does not knowingly collect information from anyone.

Changes

If this policy changes, the updated version will be posted on this page with a new date.
